# Project Metrics

These are capability-based portfolio metrics (not business impact claims).

## Core governance scope metrics
- **5 demo user roles**: Admin, HR, IT, Security, and GRC.
- **3 core lifecycle workflows**: onboarding, transfer, and offboarding.
- **5+ lifecycle governance risk signals modeled**.
- **8+ audit evidence fields captured** per lifecycle event.
- **10+ governance states modeled** across request, approval, provisioning, review, evidence, and closure.
- **100% synthetic demo data** used in the local environment.
- **Local Docker Compose demo environment** for repeatable reviewer walkthroughs.

## Why these metrics are defensible
- They describe modeled platform capability and demo coverage.
- They do not claim production ROI or real-world reduction in risk/time/cost.
- They align with local, synthetic, portfolio demonstration scope.
